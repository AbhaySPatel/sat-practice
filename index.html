How to work these

Meaning and transitions: find the signal word — although, but, despite, in contrast, because, overturned — and decide which way the sentence turns before you read a single choice. Predict your own answer, then look.

Grammar: read each half on its own and ask can this stand alone? Two complete sentences need a period, a semicolon, or a comma plus and / but / or / nor / for / so / yet. A comma alone is never enough.
